# Earth imagery

The day textures are resized, July 2004 Blue Marble Next Generation composites with topography, from NASA's Earth Observatory. [NASA dataset](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/) · [Original global JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/july/world.topo.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg)

The night textures use the 2016 NASA Black Marble global color composite from Suomi NPP VIIRS. The downsampling preserves warm light peaks for small screens. [NASA dataset](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/) · [Original global JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg)

Both are US government Earth imagery. The lighting in the map is illustrative. [NASA image use guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
