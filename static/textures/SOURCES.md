# Earth imagery

The day textures are resized, July 2004 Blue Marble Next Generation composites with topography, from NASA's Earth Observatory. [NASA dataset](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/) · [Original global JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/july/world.topo.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg)

The night textures use the 2016 NASA Black Marble global color composite from Suomi NPP VIIRS. The downsampling preserves warm light peaks for small screens. [NASA dataset](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/) · [Original global JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg)

Both are US government Earth imagery. The lighting in the map is illustrative. [NASA image use guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

The 4096 × 2048 WebPs use those same day and night originals. The cloud masks are luminance resamples of the 2002 Blue Marble cloud mosaic by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. [Blue Marble imagery and credits](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/) · [Original cloud mosaic](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/content-feature/bluemarble/images/cloud_combined_2048.jpg)

The surface maps encode east and north terrain slopes and an ocean mask derived from the 5400 × 2700 GEBCO_08 elevation and bathymetry rasters. Imagery by Jesse Allen, NASA Earth Observatory, using GEBCO data produced by the British Oceanographic Data Centre. GEBCO places the data in the public domain and requests attribution. [NASA source rasters](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/topography-bathymetry-maps/) · [GEBCO terms](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry/terms-of-use)

`orbit-sky.webp` is an original procedural starfield for Watchpost, generated from seeded point stars and multiscale noise. It is distributed under the [repository MIT license](../../LICENSE).
